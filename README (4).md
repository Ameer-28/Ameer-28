## About Me

I am **Muhammad Amin Murtadho**, a backend developer specializing in **NestJS, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL**.

Currently studying Software Engineering (RPL) at SMK Telkom Malang (2024–2027), I focus on building reliable, well-documented REST APIs with clean, type-safe, and scalable architecture. I'm open to internship opportunities.
